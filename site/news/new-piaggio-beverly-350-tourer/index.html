New Piaggio Beverly 350 Tourer

Excellent quality / price ratio for the new Piaggio Beverly 350 Tourer, with a powerful engine and equipped with standard windshield, top case and "Piaggio Mia" communication system.

by Fabio MontiniJuly 21, 2020

The Beverly series concept convinces with its brilliant driving performance, attractive design and high level of safety - not only thanks to the large wheels, but also due to its motorcycle-inspired chassis, which offers excellent driving stability at any speed. The latest flagship of the Beverly range, the Piaggio Beverly 350 Tourer, is characterized by extremely generous basic equipment and the performance of the reference engine. The Tourer comes standard with a smoked sports windshield and a capacious 36-liter top box in vehicle color. The back of the top box is matched to the color of the saddle. The basic equipment also includes the PIAGGIO MIA connectivity system, thanks to which the smartphone can be connected to the scooter via Bluetooth. In this way the user can view a wide range of information about the journey and the operating status of the vehicle via the special app, as well as use the GPS navigation.

The Beverly 350 Tourer is also equipped with an ABS brake system and ASR traction control. A remote control to open the saddle and locate the vehicle via the activated direction indicators ("Bike Finder" function) is also standard. The large compartment under the saddle offers space for two helmets. The glove box contains a USB interface for charging and connecting electronic devices. LED daytime running lights and brake lights make the Beverly Tourer particularly safe to drive. It is available in the exclusive "Materia Gray" pastel color. Combined with a double-upholstered black seat with a silver finish that extends to the passenger footrests. The equipment is completed by black lacquered rims with diamond edges and black 3D emblems.

Discover all the current Piaggio models on: www.piaggio.ch
