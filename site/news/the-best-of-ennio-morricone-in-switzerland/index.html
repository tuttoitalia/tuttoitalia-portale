The best of Ennio Morricone in Switzerland

Morricone made himself immortal about 50 years ago with his contribution to the film "Once Upon a Time in the West". A tribute to the late maestro and his most famous works is planned for 2022 with the European tour "The Best of Ennio Morricone" (five shows in Switzerland)

by Norberto CioffiMay 31, 2021

Over 100 musicians and singers from the Milan Festival Opera under the direction of famous conductor Marco Seco will be on stage and bring Ennio Morricone's upbeat music to life. The technically complex screen animation with the original scenes from the film condenses the captivating atmosphere to infinity and brings the famous goosebumps effect, which no one can escape! Ennio Morricone has created the appropriate music for around 500 films ... An impressive number! The Italo Western cinematographic genre was particularly close to his heart, having written the music for 30 such films. His film scores for the classic spaghetti western Zwei gloriges Scoundrels (Original: Il buono, il brutto, il cattivo), for the western epic Spiel mir das Lied von Tod, for the drama Mission by Roland Joffé and for the film by Giuseppe Tornatore Cinema Paradiso are legendary. Morricone has often been nominated for an Oscar for his creative music, and he's received it twice: in 2007 for his lifetime achievement and in 2016 for the music for The Hateful Eight.
