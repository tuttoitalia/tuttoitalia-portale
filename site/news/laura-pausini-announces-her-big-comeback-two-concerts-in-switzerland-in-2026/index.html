Laura Pausini announces her big comeback: two concerts in Switzerland in 2026

Laura Pausini returns to the stage with the new world tour “Io Canto / Yo Canto World Tour 2026/2027”, following the release of the single La mia storia tra le dita and the cover album Io Canto 2. The Italian artist, an international icon with more than 75 million records sold, will perform in Switzerland on October 15, 2026 at Zurich’s Hallenstadion and on November 1, 2026 at the Geneva Arena.

by Cirano TondiMay 5, 2026

Laura Pausini is ready to return to the stage with a project that bridges past and future. After the release of her new single La mia storia tra le dita, the Italian singer announced the cover album Io Canto 2 / Yo Canto 2 as well as a new world tour that will light up the most important stages around the globe throughout 2026 and 2027.

The “Io Canto / Yo Canto World Tour 2026/2027” will be the artist’s eleventh international tour and will kick off on March 27, 2026 in Pamplona, Spain. It will then travel across Europe, Latin America, the United States and Canada, before reaching major stadiums in Brazil and Europe. Good news for Swiss fans: Laura Pausini will perform on Thursday, October 15, 2026 at Zurich’s Hallenstadion and on Sunday, November 1, 2026 at the Geneva Arena. Tickets will be available starting Saturday, September 13, 2025 at 11:00 am on Ticketcorner.

“For me, concerts are the most important part of any project – because the true goal of every release is the audience,” Pausini explains. “I want to visit as many cities as possible and present a show that represents me one hundred percent.”

Italians.ch is the official media partner of the concerts in Switzerland and has supported Laura Pausini since 2008, officially as media partner since her 2012 tour, the “Inedito World Tour”.

During the tour, the singer will perform her greatest hits along with songs from the new album, which will be released in several languages – including Spanish – and will also feature reinterpretations of works by Latin American artists. This project follows in the footsteps of the acclaimed Io Canto (2006), which earned her the first Grammy Award of her career and the legendary pioneer concert at San Siro Stadium in front of 70,000 fans.

With more than 75 million records sold and over 6 billion streams, Laura Pausini is the most listened-to Italian singer worldwide. The first and only Italian artist ever to win a Grammy Award, she has also earned four Latin Grammys, a Golden Globe, and nominations for both the Emmy and the Academy Awards. In 2023, the Latin Recording Academy honored her as Person of the Year.
