Italy opens the European Football Championship in Rome. Where can you see the games in Zurich?

Originally planned for 2020, the European Football Championship was postponed to 2021 due to the pandemic. It starts on Friday, June 11th, at the Olimpico in Rome with the opening match between Italy and Turkey.

by La RedazioneJune 7, 2021

21 game days, exactly one month, from June 11th to July 11th. The games will not be played in a single country, but will be played in eleven different European cities to mark the 60th anniversary of the tournament (which has now turned sixty-one).

From Rome we will move from city to city. Amsterdam, Baku, Bucharest, Budapest, Copenhagen, Glasgow, Munich, St. Petersburg, Seville and finally London are the venues, in the latter the semi-finals and the final will be held at Wembley Stadium.

Discover all the games. The complete UEFA calendar..

For Italy it will be the first major tournament after five summers without commitments. The last game was played on July 2, 2016 on penalties against Germany in the quarter-finals of the European Championship in France. "Our national team was born after the disappointment in Russia in 2018 and the young people brought joy to the fans. We hope that the adventure will last as long as possible and that the Italians will get emotions," explains coach Roberto Mancini. "Here is the list of 26th Conveners for the tournament ".

Where can you see the games in Zurich?

Ristorante Al 21

In the typical Italian restaurant in the heart of Zurich, you can watch all the national team games and others (in accordance with UEFA regulations). Enjoy an Italian aperitif, a summer salad or reserve your table and cheer with your friends. Table reservations - Tel. 044 492 21 21

Terrace Komplex 457 (Shisha bar)

Watch all the outdoor games from the sofas of the Komplex 457 Terrace, with Italian snacks and Disaronno aperitifs. Reserve your lounge and, if you want, your shisha.

Réservation également possible directement via whatsapp : +41 76 499 04 57
