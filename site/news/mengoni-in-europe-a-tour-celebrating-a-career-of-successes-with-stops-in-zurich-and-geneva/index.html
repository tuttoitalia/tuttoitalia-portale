Mengoni in Europe: a tour celebrating a career of successes, with stops in Zurich and Geneva

Italians.ch presents the new tour of "Marco Mengoni - Live in Europe 2025" in Geneva on November 19th and in Zurich on November 24th

by Giulia GrecoMarch 10, 2025

After a triumphant 2023 culminating with his victory at the Sanremo Festival and a spectacular concert at the Circo Massimo in Rome, Italian singer-songwriter Marco Mengoni announces an extensive European tour for 2025, confirming an extraordinarily successful artistic journey. With a career spanning over 15 years, 85 platinum records, 2.9 billion audio/video streams and 10 tours under his belt, Mengoni confirms himself as a leading figure in the Italian and international music scene.

The tour will kick off with a series of concerts in Italian stadiums (the MARCO NEGLI STADI 2025, which starts on 21 June in Lignano Sabbiadoro and ends on 23 and 24 July in Messina), and will then extend to Europe. This new chapter in his musical journey will be the occasion for a full-scale performance that will represent the culmination of the sold-out 2023 tour, which culminated in the extraordinary concert at the Circo Massimo in Rome.

The victory at the Sanremo Festival 2023 with "Due Vite" (which reached 135 million views on YouTube and 339 million audio/video streams, obtaining 6 platinum records in Italy and 1 in Switzerland), participation in the Eurovision Song Contest (where he placed fourth, winning the award for "Best Composition"), and the release of his latest single, "Mandare tutto all'aria", available on all digital platforms from November 29. The 2025 European tour therefore presents itself as an important event for all lovers of quality music, an opportunity to witness an artist who continues to confirm his exceptional artistic ability and his extraordinary popularity.

Concerts in Switzerland

Marco Mengoni
Wednesday, November 19, 2025 - 8 p.m.
Arena, Geneva

Marco Mengoni
Monday, November 24, 2025 - 8 p.m.
