I segreti del mestiere (The secrets of the trade) now in the cinema

MFD Morandini Film Distribution is pleased to announce the release of the film "Les Secrets du Commerce d'Andreas Maciocci".

by La RedazioneOctober 12, 2020

I SEGRETI DEL MESTIERE (THE SECRETS OF TRADE) by Andreas Maciocci

with Alessandro Haber, Massimiliano Motta, Christo Jivkov, Igor Horvat, Fausto Maria Sciarada, Diego Benzoni, Caterina Bertone, Anna Ferzetti, Sonia Gessner, Giorgio Gobbi, Jasmin Mattei, Cinzia Morandi, Alice Papes Raeber, Soraya Sala, Arianna Scommegna

Plot

In a town on the border of Italian Switzerland, the hottest summer in recent years is the backdrop for the growth of Samuel, a 15-year-old struggling with the most important moment of his young life.

In a town on the border of Italian Switzerland, the hottest summer in recent years saw the growth of Samuel, a 15-year-old struggling with the most important moment of his young life. Samuel is an introverted boy who channeled his inspiration and his need for communication into drawing. He invented the character of Aline, a lively and determined girl who solves cases that even the police cannot solve. He talks to her in an uninterrupted ping-pong table between fantasy and reality. Determined to face a doubt that torments him and prevents him from fully living his age and his talent, he hires a private detective. With him, he embarks on a daring adventure that leads him to tackle a deeply hidden wound and discover the painful truth - linked to his family's past - that his father had hidden from him until this day.

Film produced by: Rough Cat in collaboration with Amka Film and RSI

Where and when

in theaters from November 5th:

  • Cinestar Lugano
  • Multisala Teatro Mendrisio
  • Palacinema Locarno
  • Forum Bellinzona
