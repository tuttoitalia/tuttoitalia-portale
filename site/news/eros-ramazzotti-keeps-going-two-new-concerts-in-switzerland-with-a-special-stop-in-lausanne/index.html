Eros Ramazzotti keeps going: two new concerts in Switzerland, with a special stop in Lausanne

The success of the sold-out Swiss dates in 2026 wasn't enough: Eros adds Zurich and Lausanne to the 2027 calendar. The world tour celebrating 40 years of career will stop in Zurich on 20.04.2027 and in Lausanne on 18.05.2027.

by La RedazioneApril 30, 2026

Great excitement for Italian fans in Switzerland: Eros Ramazzotti has announced two new concerts as part of his Una Storia Importante world tour. After the overwhelming success of the March 2026 Swiss dates, the Roman artist will return to the Hallenstadion in Zurich on April 20, 2027, and to the Vaudoise aréna in Lausanne on May 18, 2027.

This isn't the first time the tour has touched Switzerland: on March 16, 2026, Eros already captivated the audience at Zurich's Hallenstadion, followed eight days later by the March 24 concert at the Arena in Geneva. The Geneva evening left a lasting impression on those who were there: a show tracing 40 years of career with a setlist perfectly balanced between beloved classics and more recent tracks, a visually spectacular stage, and moments of genuine connection between artist and audience. The concert ended with the entire arena singing in unison to Più bella cosa, to lengthy applause and tearful eyes. Such was the success that the 2027 Zurich date sold out immediately, making the announcement of a new stop in Lausanne necessary.

The Una Storia Importante World Tour is more than a concert tour: it is an emotional journey through decades of music, which launched on February 14, 2026 at the Accor Arena in Paris and is set to visit over 30 countries across Europe, North America and Latin America.

With 16 studio albums, sold-out world tours, over 80 million records sold and more than 9 billion global streams, Ramazzotti ranks among the most successful Italian artists worldwide. His breakthrough came in 1984 with a victory at the Sanremo Festival with Terra Promessa, followed by hits like Adesso tu, Cose della vita and the duet with Cher Più che puoi. In February 2024, he was the guest of honour at the 74th Sanremo Festival, celebrating 40 years of career. Italians.ch is the official media partner for the Swiss dates.
