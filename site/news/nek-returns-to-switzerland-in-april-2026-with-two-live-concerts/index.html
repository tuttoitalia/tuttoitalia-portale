NEK returns to Switzerland in April 2026 with two live concerts

After a successful summer tour in Italy with NEK HITS – LIVE 2025, the popular Italian singer announces a brand new European concert tour. The European Tour 2026 will also include two exclusive dates in Switzerland: find out when and where.

by Antonio De LucaJuly 25, 2025

After a successful summer tour in Italy with NEK HITS – LIVE 2025, the popular Italian singer announces a brand new European concert tour. The European Tour 2026 will also include two exclusive dates in Switzerland:

  • Saturday, April 11, 2026 at XTRA in Zurich
  • Sunday, April 12, 2026 at Bierhübeli in Bern

Tickets go on sale Wednesday, July 30, 2025 at 2:00 PM via ticketcorner.ch.

The greatest hits from a 30-year career

With more than ten million records sold, 18 studio albums, and a musical journey spanning over three decades, NEK will take his audience through an unforgettable set of his most iconic songs. Fan favourites like Laura non c’è, Fatti avanti amore, Ci sei tu and his moving rendition of Se telefonando from Sanremo 2015 will all be part of the experience.

After the success of his anniversary album 5030, the reissue of his discography on vinyl, and the YouTube mini-series NEK HITS, this live tour is yet another confirmation of his deep connection with fans.

Italians.ch: a love story with NEK since 2011

Italians.ch is proud to be the official media partner of the Zurich concert.
This will mark the eleventh time we partner with NEK for a concert in Switzerland – a collaboration that began back in 2011 in Zurich and has continued in cities like Montreux, Geneva, Lugano, Locarno, Basel, and Biel.

A journey filled with music, passion, and a constant presence alongside one of the most beloved Italian artists of our time.

Info and tickets
