Zucchero sets the Hallenstadion ablaze: Italians.ch is there, yesterday and today

Italians.ch was present as the event's official media partner, with its own stand that welcomed many visitors and fans. A presence that returns again this evening, Thursday 18 June, for the second Zurich date of the tour, once more at the Hallenstadion. See the photos

by Antonio De LucaJune 19, 2026

The Hallenstadion in Zurich lit up last night with the unmistakable voice of Zucchero, who brought his World Tour 2026 to Switzerland. Before a red-hot crowd, the master of Italian rock blues delivered more than two hours of great classics, from “Senza una donna” to “Baila,” confirming once again his deep bond with Swiss fans and with the Italian community.

Italians.ch was present as the official media partner of the event, with its own stand that welcomed many visitors and fans. A presence that returns again this evening, Thursday 18 June, for the second Zurich date of the tour, once more at the Hallenstadion.

For those who were there, and for anyone who wants to relive the emotions of the evening, the photographs by Luca Greco are already online: a look behind the scenes and on stage that captures all the energy of a memorable concert.

See the photos

We look forward to seeing you at our stand tonight as well, to experience together the second Zucchero night in Zurich. Italians.ch is among the official media partners for both evenings (17 and 18 June), alongside Blick, Radio 24 and Radio Argovia.

Partners we warmly thank:

To all participants in the contest held during Zucchero's concerts in Zurich, Borgoterra is giving away 7 nights in Salento (www.borgoterra.com)

Via del Gusto 28 is launching a 2-for-1 Focaccia offer for everyone who shows up with the flyer at the focacceria at Lagerstrasse 28, 8004 Zurich.
