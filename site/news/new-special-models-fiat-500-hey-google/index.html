New special models Fiat 500 Hey Google

Hey Google special models combine the technology of Google with the coolness and the iconic design of the Fiat 500 model family. On Friday June 11th, 2021 the new Fiat 500 Hey Google will be exhibited at Komplex 457 in Zurich, from 5pm to 11pm.

by La RedazioneJune 10, 2021

Fiat and Google have founded an exclusive partnership called "500 Hey Google". This is the name given to two new special models of the Fiat 500 and Fiat 500X, in which the technology of the Google Assistant meets the Fiat 500 and its world of coolness and iconic design for the first time. The special models are equipped with the Mopar Connect application and the Google assistant integrated in “My Fiat”. It can be used to establish a connection to the vehicle, even over long distances. Information about the car can be requested by voice command in order to interact with it. This requires a smartphone or the smart Google Nest Hub display, which is included in the welcome kit with which the special models of the Hey Google family are equipped. This technology enables user-friendly and innovative connectivity that makes everyday life even easier.

On Friday June 11th, 2021, the new Fiat 500 Hey Google will be on display at Komplex 457 in Zurich, from 5 p.m. to 11 p.m

One of the secrets of the Fiat 500's success is that it is always up to date. And now the special models of the Hey Google series are opening another new chapter in the history of the model family. Interaction with the vehicle is possible anywhere, even if you are not sitting in the vehicle but, for example, on the sofa at home. All you have to do is activate the system with the voice command "Hey Google, ask MyFiat ..." to find out, for example, where the vehicle is parked, how much fuel is left in the tank, what the address of the nearest authorized workshop is or what the status is the trip odometer shows. You can also tell from a distance whether the car doors or the trunk are locked.

The Fiat 500X Hey Google is also available in the two-tone Gelato White paintwork with a black roof. 16-inch alloy wheels, fog lights and darkened window panes at the rear give the Italian crossover an urban look. The interior has been upgraded with newly designed seats, the silver dashboard insert with the white 500 logo, soft-touch steering wheel with multifunctional buttons, height-adjustable driver's seat and front armrest. The standard equipment also includes an extensive technology package, including front and rear parking sensors, reversing camera, rain and light sensor, two-zone automatic air conditioning, and the Uconnect 7 "HD touchscreen radio with navigation system and six speakers. The entertainment system is with Android Auto and Apple Car Play compatible and offers the Mopar Connect application. The engine range consists of two Euro 6D-compliant petrol engines: the Firefly series and with a displacement of 1.0 liters, they produce 88 kW (120 PS) and 110 kW (150 PS) from 1, 3 liters displacement and DDCT transmission. For the body, the colors Passione Red, Gelato White, Argento Gray, Moda Gray, Cinema Black, Italia Blue and Cinema Black are available. The new Fiat 500X Hey Google is now available in Switzerland can be ordered at prices from CHF 25,440.
