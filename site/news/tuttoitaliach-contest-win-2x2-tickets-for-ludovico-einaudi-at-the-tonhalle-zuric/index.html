Contest: Win 2 Tickets for Ludovico Einaudi at the Tonhalle Zurich

Both dates on 14.05.2026 are SOLD OUT, but with italians.ch you can win 2 tickets for the morning "Solo Piano" concert at 11.15 a.m.

by Cirano TondiMay 10, 2026

A chance you can no longer buy: only win

Both Ludovico Einaudi shows at the Tonhalle Zürich on 14.05.2026 are completely sold out. The evening concert at 8.00 p.m. went sold out in just two hours after the box office opened, prompting promoter AllBlues to add a second concert in the morning, at 11.15 a.m. That one is now sold out too. For the Turin-born pianist and composer, whose film scores for "Intouchables", "Nomadland" and "The Father" are loved worldwide and whose pieces accompany millions of listeners, this is the first time in his career that he plays twice on the same day.

For those who couldn’t get a ticket, italians.ch is launching an exclusive contest: 2 tickets up for grabs for the morning concert at 11.15 a.m.

Solo Piano, unamplified, inside the Tonhalle

Both performances on 14.05.2026 are presented in an unamplified solo piano format. The Tonhalle Zürich hall, with its intimate acoustics and refined elegance, hosts a programme that travels through the composer’s artistic path: minimal lines, suspended melodies, a writing style that naturally blends classical tradition, contemporary sensibility and cinematic resonance. The audience sits very close to the artist, in an intimate dialogue with the pure timbre of the instrument.

The 11.15 a.m. concert, in particular, offers an unusual relationship with the time of listening. Late morning becomes a space for focus and depth, far from the usual rhythms of an evening show, and is ideal for those who want to experience music with full presence.

Italians.ch is Media Partner

Italians.ch is Media Partner of both dates and considers this double Zurich appearance one of the most anticipated cultural moments of 2026 in Switzerland. The two concerts on 14.05.2026 at the Tonhalle will be the composer’s only Swiss dates.

How to enter the contest

To enter the draw for the 2 tickets, just follow the instructions on the contest page of italians.ch. Participation is free.
