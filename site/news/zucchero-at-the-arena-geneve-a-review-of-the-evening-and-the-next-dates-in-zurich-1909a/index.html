Zucchero at the Arena Genève: a review of the evening and the next dates in Zurich

On 21 May the Arena of Geneva opened its doors to Adelmo Fornaciari's "World Tour 2026". In June the appointment moves to Zurich with two evenings at the Hallenstadion, where the last tickets are still available.

by Antonio De LucaMay 23, 2026

On Thursday 21 May 2026, the Arena Genève hosted the first Swiss leg of Zucchero's "World Tour 2026". An evening of blues, rock and Italian song built around the tracks that have made Adelmo Fornaciari one of the most loved and best-selling Italian artists in the world: more than sixty million records sold over his career, including eight million copies of the album "Oro, incenso & birra" alone. On stage, the classics such as "Senza una donna", "Diamante" and "Baila (Sexy Thing)" were not missing, alternated with the more recent tracks of the repertoire.

The Geneva audience, largely made up of the Italian-speaking community of French-speaking Switzerland, responded warmly to a setlist designed to span forty years of career. Italians.ch was present as official media partner of the Swiss leg of the tour and documented the evening: the photos of the concert are available in our dedicated gallery.

See all the photos

For those who could not make it to Geneva, the "World Tour 2026" also stops in Zurich with a double appointment at the Hallenstadion: Wednesday 17 and Thursday 18 June 2026, both evenings with doors opening at 6:30 PM and showtime at 8:00 PM. For both dates, the last tickets are still available on Ticketcorner: 17 June date and 18 June date.

In Zurich too, Italians.ch will be present as official media partner, with an information stand set up on both evenings at the Hallenstadion. It will be the opportunity to meet the editorial team, pick up information material and exchange a few words before the concert.
