Radio my105 achieves 2 million stream views per month

The success story continues: Last year my105 was able to increase its stream views by 74%. The music app has thus recorded extremely strong growth for the fifth year in a row.

by La RedazioneJanuary 14, 2021

The numbers are impressive and make my105 one of the most successful new launches in the Swiss media landscape.

  • My105 now has over 2 million stream views per month
  • An increase of 74% compared to the previous year
  • The average listening time is 70 minutes.

Since November 2019 my105 has also been on the air with its “105 DJ Radio” program in the largest cities in German-speaking Switzerland via DAB + and has so far reached around 3.6 million potential listeners. This range will also be expanded significantly in 2021 with the activation in other areas (e.g. Bernese Oberland, Valais, Southeastern Switzerland, Ticino) by 700,000 to 4.3 million potential listeners.
