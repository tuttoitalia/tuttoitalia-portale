Cecchetto introduces the new Faber Diabolik® Limited Edition coffee machine

To coincide with the theatrical release of the long-awaited feature film by the Manetti brothers, inspired by Angela Giussani's famous comic book Diabolik, Cecchetto Import AG, the official distributor of Faber products for Switzerland, is pleased to present the new Diabolik-themed limited edition coffee machines. Faber's essential and sophisticated line is linked to Diabolik's timeless appeal.

by Antonio De LucaFebruary 10, 2022

To coincide with the theatrical release of the long-awaited feature film by the Manetti brothers, inspired by Angela Giussani's famous comic book Diabolik, Cecchetto Import AG, the official distributor of Faber products for Switzerland, is pleased to present the new Diabolik-themed limited edition coffee machines. Faber's essential and sophisticated line is linked to Diabolik's timeless appeal.

Faber Italia has produced a limited edition of only 2000 pieces with different subjects and colors, a new line of coffee machines with an exclusive design and characterized by the "Diabolik Edition" logo.

Visit the website www.coffee-excellence.ch and buy this piece of Italian comics history.
