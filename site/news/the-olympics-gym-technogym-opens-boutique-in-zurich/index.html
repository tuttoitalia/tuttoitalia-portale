The Olympics gym, Technogym, opens boutique in Zurich

The Italian company Technogym, world leader in wellness and official supplier of the last seven Olympics, inaugurates its new Swiss boutique in Zurich on Saturday 17 March 2018

by Gloria BressanMarch 17, 2018

Space for sport. From the claim of Technogym - "The Wellness Company" the company of wellbeing - we can deduce the whole philosophy of a great brand that has made the history of wellness. «Technogym launched the concept of wellness and exported it to the best wellness centers, hotels, companies, private homes and medical centers all over the world» explains Nerio Alessandri, Founder and President of the same. Founded in 1983, Technogym immediately began exporting its products to nearby Switzerland "which is currently the first international market in which 30 years ago - continues Alessandri - we began exporting our products outside Italy and today, as a leader market, we are very proud to inaugurate a retail outlet in Zurich, certainly a point of reference for wellness throughout the Confederation».

Technogym Zurich will extend over an area of ​​over 150 square meters spread over 2 floors. It is a place designed to live the Wellness lifestyle in which not only to buy the best products, technologies and services for physical exercise at home, but also to have access to complete advice from experienced and interior personal trainers designer. The real protagonists of the space, however, will be the Personal Line products, the Technogym design icon developed in collaboration with Antonio Citterio who combines designer design with cutting-edge technology and includes treadmills, exercise bikes, elliptical and Kinesis; Artis, the top collection of the Technogym range, for those who want to create a real professional gym, consisting of cardio, strength and functional products, connected and coordinated in terms of style and design; MyCycling, the new indoor training solution dedicated to cyclists which, thanks to connectivity, allows you to carry out a completely personalized training and finally Skillmill, Skillrow and Skillrun products for athletic performance training launched also thanks to the collaboration with the best athletes at world of the last seven Olympics.

«The practice of physical activity at home is no longer a luxury for a few, but a pleasure for many» says Andrea Matteucci, CEO of Fimex Distribution AG, Technogym's exclusive distributor for Switzerland. «Our products - he reiterated - are design furniture that decorate and embellish our habitat. More and more customers have for example a fitness room at home and at the same time are subscribers to a club ». The opening of the Zurich store is part of an overall project that sees the direct presence of Technogym in the main cities of the world. The points in Milan, New York, Madrid, Dubai, Mexico City, Vienna, Moscow and the dedicated space inside the Harrod's department store in London are already operational.

History of a great Italian brand
‍
Founded in 1983, Technogym is the world leader in the supply of technologies, services and design products for the Fitness, Wellness and Sport sectors. Technogym offers a complete range of cardio, strength and functional training equipment, as well as a digital cloud platform that allows users to connect to their profiles and access their personalized wellness experience anywhere, both through Technogym products and through mobile devices. The company now has around 2,000 employees at 14 branches in Europe, the United States, Asia, the Middle East, Australia and South America and exports 90% of its production to over 100 countries. Technogym has equipped 80,000 Wellness centers and over 200,000 homes around the world. Technogym has been the official supplier of the last seven editions of the Olympic Games: Sydney 2000, Athens 2004, Turin 2006, Beijing 2008, London 2012, Rio 2016 and Pyeongchang 2018.
