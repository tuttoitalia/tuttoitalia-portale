Ligabue brings “Certe Notti in Europa” to Zurich

Italians.ch Is Official Media Partner for the Major Swiss Live Return. A must‑see event for fans of Italian music: Luciano Ligabue is returning to Switzerland for a single show.

by Federica LupinuJune 26, 2025

A must‑see event for fans of Italian music: Luciano Ligabue is returning to Switzerland for a single show. On Saturday, 16 May 2026, he will perform live at THE HALL in Zurich as part of his new “Certe Notti in Europa” tour. The concert promises a night bursting with energy, emotion, and timeless hits, and Italians.ch is proud to be the official media partner.

Thirty‑five Years of Music in One Show

The tour celebrates two milestones: twenty years since the legendary Campovolo concert in 2005 – Europe’s largest outdoor show with over 165,000 attendees – and thirty years since the cult album “Buon Compleanno Elvis” released in 1995 that cemented Ligabue’s rockstar status. Hits like “Certe Notti”, with nearly 70 million Spotify streams, guarantee an unforgettable concert experience.

A European Tour with a Single Swiss Stop

“Kerte Notti in Europa” kicks off on 1 May in Barcelona and travels through eight European cities including Madrid, Paris, Brussels, and Luxembourg. The Zurich show on 16 May 2026 is the only Swiss date, offering a unique opportunity to experience Ligabue live.

Tickets on Sale from 27 June 2025

Tickets go on sale Friday, 27 June 2025 at 10:00 via Ticketcorner.ch. The ticket includes public transport access across all ZVV zones (2nd class) on the concert day, a practical and eco‑friendly feature.
