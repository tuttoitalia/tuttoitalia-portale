Iva Zanicchi, The Italian Tenors and the legendary New Trolls live in Biel

Biel gets ready to light up the winter with the new edition of the Christmas Sessions, scheduled from November 20 to 23, 2025. Four nights of music, each with its own soul and unique protagonists, set to thrill audiences with emotions, major hits, and international vibes.

by Antonio De LucaJune 18, 2025

Four nights, four musical worlds and one all-Italian evening

Biel/Bienne is preparing for the 2025 Christmas Sessions, taking place from November 20 to 23. Each night will offer a unique musical experience filled with passion, global influences, and beloved classics.

Opening the festival on Thursday, November 20, is pop legend Thomas Anders, the iconic voice of Modern Talking. With hits like You're My Heart, You're My Soul and Cheri Cheri Lady, he’ll take the audience on a nostalgic journey through the 1980s.

On Friday, November 21, Bligg takes the stage – one of Switzerland's most influential artists. Known for tracks like Rosalie and Mänsch, he’ll bring energy, rhythm, and unmistakable Swiss authenticity.

Saturday, November 22: Italy’s night, a tribute to passion and melody

The emotional highlight of the festival comes on Saturday, November 22, with a night dedicated to Italian music. On stage:

  • Iva Zanicchi, “the voice of Italy”, with timeless songs like Zingara and La riva del garda
  • The legendary New Trolls, blending rock, progressive, and classical music
  • The elegant trio The Italian Tenors, fusing opera, tradition, and a modern twist

Together, these three acts represent the passion, diversity, and cultural richness of Italian music – a moving journey from classical melody to contemporary charm.

More info

Location: Biel/Bienne, Palais des Congrès
Dates: November 20–23, 2025
