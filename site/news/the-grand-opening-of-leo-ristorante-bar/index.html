The Grand Opening of Leo Ristorante & Bar

LEO Opening Party: Thursday, March 17, from 5:00 p.m Above the rooftops of Zurich's Bahnhofstrasse, Ristorante LEO brings you closer to the Italian way of life day by day.

by Federica LupinuMarch 17, 2022

WELCOME TO LEO! The Grand Opening of Leo Ristorante & Bar is in full swing this evening If you are looking for la Dolce Vita, you don't have to travel far. Passion, authenticity and hospitality - just as we know Italy – discover it now at LEO, the new restaurant on the 5th floor at Globus Zurich Bahnhofstrasse. From morning to night, the restaurant spoils its guests with true classics, such as antipasti, homemade pasta and grilled specialties, accompanied by a wide selection of wines and cocktails from all regions of Italy.

LEO Opening Party: Thursday, March 17, from 5:00 p.m

If you are out and about this evening, come by and enjoy a cocktail above the rooftops of Zurich. We are open until 2am tonight with a live band, DJ and other fun surprises to round up the celebrations. We are looking forward to your visit!
