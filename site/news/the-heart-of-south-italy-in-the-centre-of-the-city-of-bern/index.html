Casa Sana: conscious taste in the heart of Breitenrain

Between olive oil and hemp-based cosmetics, between clear visions and authentic flavors, a new space has opened in Bern: Casa Sana, the new shop-in-shop in the Breitenrain district, is much more than just a store. It’s an invitation to live more consciously, with taste and authenticity.

Here, it’s not just about what we put on our plate or our skin, but above all how and why we choose what surrounds us. Casa Sana offers products with a soul, carefully selected, ethically made, and deeply connected to a philosophy that values true, no-frills quality.

The heart of the South in the center of the city

In the urban heart of Bern but with a Mediterranean soul, Casa Sana tells stories through its products: stories of centuries-old olive trees, wild herbs, golden grapes, and unspoiled lands. A little corner of the South filled with authenticity.

The selection is broad and surprising:

  • Pruna offers the power of the land in artisanal distillates from Serbia.
  • Sannabis brings nature’s calm into everyday life with hemp cosmetics that are gentle yet effective—perfect for hydrating, soothing, and regenerating the skin.
  • Engine, the organic Italian gin, delivers an explosion of aromas through maceration and the energy of Mediterranean botanicals.
  • There are also culinary specialties from Italy, Spain, and France: preserves, oils, sauces, and delicacies that turn any kitchen into a little Southern bistro—with no exaggeration, just heart.

A simple philosophy: less, but better

Casa Sana is for those who choose with their heart. For those who want fewer things, but of greater value. For those who seek authenticity and want to know what they bring into their body and life. It’s a place of inspiration and discovery, where every purchase tells a story of respect, territory, and passion.

Come and discover it

Casa Sana awaits you with a wide selection of products to taste and fall in love with. On opening days, there will be snacks, refreshing drinks, and special offers to celebrate this new adventure together.
