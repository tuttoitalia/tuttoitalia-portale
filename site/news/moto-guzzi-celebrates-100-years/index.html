Moto Guzzi celebrates 100 years

1921 - 2021 a century of motorcycling history always looking ahead, with the ability to innovate while remaining faithful to its values and traditions. Many initiatives by Mato Guzzi to celebrate the century, which will culminate with WYD - Moto Guzzi World Days, scheduled in Mandello del Lario (LC) from 9 to 12 September.

by Fabio MontiniMarch 28, 2021

Mandello del Lario (LC- Italy), March 2021 - On March 15, 2021 Moto Guzzi celebrates its first and legendary 100 years.
A century of history, of splendid motorcycles, of victories, of adventures, of extraordinary characters who have built the myth of the Eagle brand. Moto Guzzi is celebrating this exceptional birthday just as it is experiencing a new youth.

The motorcycle range is profoundly renewed while maintaining the brand's values of style and authenticity intact, combined with the best technical equipment in terms of electronic driving aids and every single Moto Guzzi is built in the Mandello del Lario factory with craftsmanship. Each model is created in compliance with a unique and authentic identity, wisely balancing classic style with cutting-edge technology and reflecting a construction philosophy that creates an exclusive relationship between bike and rider.

In its hundred years Moto Guzzi has signed victories on circuits all over the world, taking the Italian flag to 14 World Titles.

Moto Guzzi has always been loved and respected by motorcyclists from all over the world, whatever their vehicle, and motorcyclists will be the protagonists of the WYD - Moto Guzzi World Days which, in Mandello del Lario from 9 to 12 September, will be the moment highest of celebrations in the centenary year, the biggest party for such a special birthday.

WYDs have always been an unmissable event for every enthusiast and, highly anticipated, they return ten years after the last edition. Tens of thousands will flock from all over the world to experience a unique and unforgettable event made possible thanks to the collaboration between Moto Guzzi, the International Motorcycle Rally Committee and the municipality of Mandello del Lario (LC-Italy)
