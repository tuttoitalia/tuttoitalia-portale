Zucchero Announces Two Concerts in Switzerland in 2026

Italians.ch presents Zucchero's new tour: the Italian icon returns to Switzerland to delight fans with two unmissable concerts in Geneva and Zurich.

by Federica LupinuApril 21, 2025

The renowned Italian singer-songwriter Zucchero is set to return to Switzerland for two can't-miss concerts. The first stop is scheduled for Thursday, May 21, 2026, at 8 PM at the Arena in Geneva, followed by a second performance on Thursday, June 18, 2026, at Hallenstadion in Zurich. Both events promise to celebrate his iconic music and extraordinary talent.

Known for his powerful voice and distinctive style blending blues, rock, and Italian melodies, Zucchero has captivated global audiences with timeless hits like “Senza una donna,” “Baila (Sexy Thing),” and “Diamante.” These concerts will feature beloved classics alongside new material, offering fans a deeply engaging, emotional experience.

The Zurich event is presented by Italians.ch as the official media partner—a testament to the growing international support for Italian artists. Tickets for both shows will be available soon, so fans should stay tuned to secure their seats at these unforgettable evenings.

The Arena Geneva and Hallenstadion Zurich serve as prestigious venues, providing the perfect atmosphere for Zucchero's performance. With his charisma and audience connection, he promises an evening of heartfelt music and memorable moments.
