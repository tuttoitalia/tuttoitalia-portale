AMITALIA Italian art and culture in Switzerland presents four first-run event films

From January 24 to February 14, 2021, the Italian cinema and culture festival will take place with four documentary films in exclusive preview for Switzerland.

by Cirano TondiJanuary 20, 2021

Celebrated anniversaries

From January 24 to February 14, 2021, the Italian cinema and culture festival will take place with four documentary films in exclusive preview for Switzerland.

W MARIA MONTESSORI‍

Sunday 24 January 2021, 6:30 pm (accessible until 00:30 am)
Directed by Emanuela Audisio and Malina De Carlo, Italy, 2020, 75'
Celebrates and describes the teaching method of Maria Montessori the documentary made for the 150th anniversary of birth of a woman who, with her revolutionary pedagogical method, aimed at education towards freedom, has become a world figure: Maria Montessori.

LEONARDO SCIASCIA - SCRITTORE ALIENO

Sunday 31 January 2021, 6.30 pm (accessible until 00:30 am)
Directed by Simona Risi, Italy, 2020, 78'
A hundred years after the birth of Leonardo Sciascia, a reading was needed that reflected his character and stylistic choices. That's what the documentary does.

GLI INDIFFERENTI

Sunday 7 February 2021, 6:30 pm (accessible until 00:30 am)
Directed by Leonardo Guerra Seragnoli, Italy, 2020, 81'
The film is the new film adaptation of the famous and homonymous novel by the Roman writer Alberto Moravia, published in 1929 and which made the history of our literature.

IL NOSTRO EDUARDO

Sunday 14 February 2021, 6.30 pm (accessible until 00:30 am)
Directed by Didi Gnocchi, Italy, 2020
The great artist is presented for the first time by his family, his grandchildren, the sons of Luca, Matteo, Tommaso and Luisella.
