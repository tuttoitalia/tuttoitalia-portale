The Gianna Nannini European Tour 2022 also runs through Zurich and Geneva

The big return of the international Gianna has just been announced. On her European tour planned for 2022, there will also be live dates in Zurich and Geneva

by Michela Dal TosoMay 18, 2021

Gianna Nannini is back. The Italian rock star returns to Switzerland and Europe with a load of positive energy, also thanks to the songs from her current album "La Differenza" (The Difference), a pop masterpiece steeped in acoustic ballads. It is noteworthy that the new songs are completely opposite to the current musical trends. But our Gianna surprises us again and again. She retired to John McBride's recording studio in Nashville, isolated herself from the world, and recorded her album as before. Analog, acoustic, with a touch of authentic blues and rock. Gianna promises, however, that the new record fits her style perfectly: melodically charming, energetic and of course with its scratchy organ as we have known it for over 40 years.
