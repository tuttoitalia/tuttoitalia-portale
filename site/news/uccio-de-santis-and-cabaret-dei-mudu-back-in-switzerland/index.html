Uccio de Santis and Cabaret dei Mudù back in Switzerland

Uccio De Santis, the undisputed king of carabinieri jokes, returns to Switzerland with three shows in Basel, Zurich and Lugano

by Giulia GrecoApril 20, 2021

Artecult is proud to announce the return of Uccio De Santis to Switzerland with the Cabaret dei Mudu '.

Known to all for his classic carabinieri jokes, the Bari comedian is accompanied by the must-sees Umberto Sardella and Antonella Genga, his two right arms who make up the Cabaret dei Mudù together. The three comedians, known on the Internet for their performances as priests, rifles or doctors, will bring their new show and will also refer to the best jokes related to Mudù’s big TV hit.

The renewed show 2021 tells the life of an actor "by vocation" in the form of a monologue and a sketch. From the beginning, overcoming shyness, organizing parties, from first love to his true great love: theater. A show that tells the present, with fun curtains between men and women on a daily basis; Holidays, shopping, the crisis. Stories in which everyone can identify and laugh heartily. The comedy also includes a moment when Uccio De Santis interacts with the audience, the true workhorse of the Puglia artist, creating comic book curtains with great commitment.
