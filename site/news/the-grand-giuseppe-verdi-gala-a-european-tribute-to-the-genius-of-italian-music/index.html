The Grand Giuseppe Verdi Gala: A European Tribute to the Genius of Italian Music

After the great success of the 2024 tour, the magic of Giuseppe Verdi returns to Switzerland with a new edition: The Grand Giuseppe Verdi Gala. This event celebrates the timeless greatness of the Maestro from Busseto, whose music continues to move and inspire audiences around the world.

by Rosa De VitaOctober 16, 2025

Giuseppe Verdi once wrote to a friend: “To copy the truth may be a good thing, but to invent it is much better.” It is precisely this pursuit of truth and authenticity that makes his works so universal—then as now.

More than two centuries after his birth, the emotions that emanate from his masterpieces remain alive, relevant, and profoundly human. Characters such as Violetta Valéry from La Traviata, Aida, or Rigoletto embody love, tragedy, passion, and inner strength, elements that find new life in this exceptional production.

The Giuseppe Verdi Gala program offers audiences the most famous pieces from Verdi’s operatic repertoire, featuring excerpts from La Traviata, Rigoletto, Aida, Il Trovatore, Nabucco, and I Vespri Siciliani. Iconic arias, duets, and ensembles will be interwoven with unforgettable choruses such as “Va pensiero” from Nabucco and the Triumphal March from Aida, accompanied by refined staging, period costumes, and original choreography.

This production is presented by Opera Polska, an orchestra and choir founded in 1989 by Maestro Marek Tracz in collaboration with Wrocław Television. The ensemble brings together some of the finest musicians and singers from major opera houses in Poland and across Europe, creating an artistic project of the highest quality.

The Giuseppe Verdi Gala is not merely a concert but a journey through Italian history and culture, a tribute to a composer who masterfully united art and civic engagement. As early as 1842, with the chorus Va pensiero, Verdi gave voice to the Italian national spirit, and in 1874, he was appointed Senator of the Kingdom of Italy for his contribution to the nation.

Following its triumph with Swiss audiences in 2024, this new edition promises to be an unforgettable experience—one that unites generations through the emotional power and timeless beauty of Verdi’s music.
